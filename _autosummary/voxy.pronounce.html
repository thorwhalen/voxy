voxy.pronounce

voxy.pronounce¶

Checking, in the audio, which reading of a flagged word a render used.

voxy.speechtext.flag_risks() lists the words a model may misread (heteronyms such as French “est” /ɛ/ vs “Est” /ɛst/). This module listens for the answer: it finds the word in a render (word timestamps from ASR), asks a phoneme recogniser for its frame-wise phoneme log-probabilities, and scores each candidate reading by its CTC likelihood. The reading the audio supports best is the one the model said; a render whose word came out as the wrong one can be re-rendered.

ASR word times are loose, so a word cropped alone carries bits of its neighbours, and a neighbour’s /s/ or /t/ then reads as the longer reading. audit() (the call for a finished render) therefore scores each reading as the whole phrase around it, the neighbours written out by espeak (phrase_readings(), espeak_phonemizer()); verify_words() and render_verified() still crop.

The recogniser is facebook/wav2vec2-lv-60-espeak-cv-ft, run in its own torch env (PHONEME_ENV, built by voxy.envs, with a bundled espeak-ng). Its vocab.json maps espeak IPA symbols to ids, so readings are written in IPA and tokenised against that vocabulary. Everything above the recogniser is pure numpy and takes the recogniser (emitter) and the phonemizer as arguments, so it is tested without torch.

>>> vocab = ["<pad>", "ɛ", "s", "t"]
>>> emissions = log_probs_from_path([1, 0, 0], vocab_size=4)  # hears /ɛ/ only
>>> best_reading(emissions, vocab, {"verb": "ɛ", "noun": "ɛst"})
'verb'

voxy.pronounce.audit(text, audio, *, readings=None, language='fr', expected=None, detectors=(), emitter=None, words_of=<function _transcribed_words>, scoring='phrase', phonemize=None, context=1, filler_penalty=1.0, padding=0.08, margin=None)[source]¶

Which reading did each heteronym of text get in the finished audio?

ASR (WER) checks cannot answer this: Whisper writes “est” whether the model said /ɛ/ or /ɛst/. This flags the words of text that have several readings (voxy.speechtext.flag_risks()), finds each in the audio (ASR word times; the n-th time the text has a word is the n-th time ASR heard it) and scores its readings with the phoneme recogniser.

Parameters:

  • text (str) – What the model was given (the speech text, not the display text).

  • audio (str | PathLike) – The render.

  • readings (Mapping[str, Mapping[str, str]] | None) – {word: {reading name: IPA}}; default heteronym_readings() for language.

  • expected (Mapping[str, str] | Callable[[object], str | None] | None) – The reading each word should have: {word: reading}, or risk -> reading to decide per occurrence (risk.line, risk.word). Otherwise what a detectors entry found for that word on that line (e.g. voxy.speechtext.pos_detector()), else None (just report).

  • detectors (Iterable[Callable[[str], Iterable]]) – text -> risks proposers of expected readings.

  • emitter (Callable[[str, float, float], tuple[ndarray, Sequence[str]]] | None) – The phoneme recogniser; default phoneme_emitter().

  • words_of (Callable[[str], Iterable[Mapping]]) – audio -> timed words; default ASR (voxy.refprep.transcribe).

  • scoring (str) – How a reading is scored in the audio. "phrase" (default): the context words on each side are written out with phonemize and each reading is scored as the whole phrase (phrase_readings()); the neighbours are the same in every candidate, so only the word decides. "spot": the reading is spotted in that window, the rest left to filler (spot_readings(); no phonemizer, but a neighbour’s /s/ or /t/ can be credited to the longer reading). "crop": the word alone, cut at its ASR times (padding each side), scored by score_readings(); ASR word times are often too loose for this.

  • phonemize (Callable[[Sequence[str], str], Sequence[str]] | None) – For "phrase"; default espeak_phonemizer().

  • context (int) – Words kept on each side of the flagged one.

  • margin (float | None) – See Verdict.decided; default DFLT_CONTEXT_MARGIN, DFLT_SPOT_MARGIN or DFLT_MARGIN by scoring.

Return type:

list[Finding]

Returns the findings in text order; [f for f in findings if f.ok is False] are the words decidedly misread (re-render those), f.ok is None ones were not decided or not found.

voxy.pronounce.checks_from_risks(risks, *, readings, expected=None)[source]¶

verify_words checks for the flagged words that have known IPA readings.

readings maps a word (case-insensitively) to {name: IPA}, e.g. from a Lexique-derived heteronym list the caller supplies; expected names the reading each word should have, and otherwise the risks’ own expected (what a detector such as voxy.speechtext.pos_detector found) is used. Words with no entry are skipped. Each word is checked once: verify_words finds every place it is spoken, so a word whose risks name different readings gets none here (group the risks by line with checks_by_line() to check each one).

voxy.pronounce.phrase_readings(log_probs, vocab, readings, *, before='', after='', blank=None)[source]¶

Each reading’s CTC log-likelihood as the whole phrase before + reading + after.

The words around the flagged one are written out (IPA) and are the same in every candidate, so the comparison isolates the word: its neighbours’ sounds cannot be credited to a longer reading, as they are when the word is cropped or spotted alone.

voxy.pronounce.render_verified(lines, checks, *, render, emitter, words_of=<function _transcribed_words>, max_attempts=3, padding=0.08, margin=0.5)[source]¶

Render each line, and re-render the ones whose flagged words came out wrong.

A line with checks is verified (verify_words()) after each render and rendered again while a word is decidedly misread (failing()), up to max_attempts renders; a line without checks is rendered once, and a blank line not at all (line numbers still count it, as flag_risks does). LineTakes.chosen is the take to keep; LineTakes.verified also requires each expected reading to be confirmed (a word ASR did not find, or the recogniser could not decide, leaves the line unverified rather than retried).

Parameters:

  • lines (Iterable[str]) – The lines to speak, in order (line numbers start at 1).

  • checks (Mapping[int, Sequence[tuple[str, Mapping[str, str], str | None]]]) – By line number, the words to check (checks_by_line()).

  • render (Callable[[str, int, int], str | PathLike]) – (text, line, attempt) -> audio path; each call should give a new take (a new seed, or a service’s own variation). speech_renderer() adapts a voxy.base.SpeechModel.

  • emitter (Callable[[str, float, float], tuple[ndarray, Sequence[str]]]) – The phoneme recogniser (phoneme_emitter()).

  • words_of (Callable[[str], Iterable[Mapping]]) – audio -> timed words; the default transcribes with voxy.refprep.transcribe.

  • max_attempts (int) – Renders of one line at most.

  • padding (float) – As in verify_words().

  • margin (float) – As in verify_words().

Return type:

list[LineTakes]

>>> heard = {"est": "ɛ"}  # a fake recogniser: what each word sounds like
>>> def emitter(audio, start, end):
...     vocab = ["<pad>", "ɛ", "s", "t"]
...     path = [vocab.index(s) for s in heard["est"]]
...     return log_probs_from_path(path, vocab_size=4), vocab
>>> def render(text, line, attempt):
...     heard["est"] = "ɛst" if attempt else "ɛ"  # the second take is right
...     return f"take-{line}-{attempt}.wav"
>>> words = lambda audio: [{"start": 0.0, "end": 0.3, "text": "Est"}]
>>> checks = {1: [("Est", {"verb": "ɛ", "noun": "ɛst"}, "noun")]}
>>> [result] = render_verified(["L'Est"], checks, render=render, emitter=emitter,
...                            words_of=words)
>>> [t.audio for t in result.takes], result.chosen.attempt, result.verified
(['take-1-0.wav', 'take-1-1.wav'], 1, True)

voxy.pronounce.spot_log_likelihood(log_probs, target, *, blank=0, filler_penalty=None)[source]¶

log P of target said somewhere in log_probs, the rest being filler.

Keyword spotting under CTC: frames before and after the target are explained by a filler that takes each frame’s best symbol at a cost of filler_penalty nats. Scoring a word inside a window that holds its neighbours, a reading wins by matching phonemes that are really there, not by the window’s edges: a crop cut from loose ASR word times otherwise favours the longer reading (/ɛst/ over /ɛ/).

>>> vocab = ["<pad>", "ɛ", "s", "t", "l"]
>>> lp = log_probs_from_path([4, 1, 0, 4], vocab_size=5)  # "l ɛ l": the verb
>>> verb, noun = (spot_log_likelihood(lp, tokenize_phonemes(r, vocab)) for r in ("ɛ", "ɛst"))
>>> verb > noun
True
Return type:

float

  • words (Iterable[Mapping]) – Timed words of the render (transcribe(audio)’s segments’ words).

  • emitter (Callable[[str, float, float], tuple[ndarray, Sequence[str]]]) – (audio, start, end) -> (log_probs, vocab); the default worker is phoneme_emitter().

  • padding (float) – Seconds added to each side of the ASR span.

  • margin (float) – See Verdict.decided.
