voxy.singing¶

Singing: sing a score with lyrics in a voice (SVS), or re-voice a sung take (SVC).

>>> import voxy
>>> voxy.sing("melody.mid", "Light a can-dle in the win-dow").save("sung.wav")
>>> voice = voxy.singing_voice("me.wav", "phrase.mid", "la la la la")
>>> voxy.sing("song.musicxml", voice=voice).save("song.wav")
>>> voxy.convert_singing("take.wav", voice=voice).save("take-in-my-voice.wav")

A singing voice is a sung clip, plus, for synthesis, the notes and words it sings (singing_voice): a zero-shot singer copies the clip’s timbre, and SoulX-Singer also aligns the clip with its notes and phonemes. The simplest way to get that alignment is to make the clip from a known score: sing a short given phrase (its MIDI or MusicXML, with its lyrics) and pass the recording with that score. Without a voice, a synthetic one is used (synthetic_singing_voice: a formant synthesiser singing “la”, which belongs to no one). Conversion needs only the clip. Only clone a singing voice with its owner’s consent; each backend records its own usage restrictions in singing_backends()[name].consent.

Scores are MIDI or MusicXML files, music21 streams, or lists of voxy.score.Note (voxy.score.read_score); lyrics are one token per note (can-dle spreads a word over two notes, - holds it one more).

Backends are SingingBackend records (singing_backends()), each running in its own environment (python -m voxy setup soulx); soulx (SoulX-Singer, Apache-2.0) is the built-in one. A model is the seam: sing(..., model=...) takes any object with sing(notes, voice, *, transpose, language, **params) and convert(audio_path, voice, *, transpose, **params) returning a Speech.

>>> class Echo:
...     name = "echo"
...     def sing(self, notes, voice, **kw):
...         return Speech(b"RIFF", "wav", "echo", text=" ".join(n.word for n in notes))
>>> from voxy.score import notes_from_pitches
>>> sing(notes_from_pitches([(60, 1), (62, 1)]), "do re", voice=VoiceProfile(
...     {"prompt_audio": "v.wav", "prompt_notes": []}, 0, "echo", 24000), model=Echo()).text
'do re'

Parameters:

  • score – A MIDI or MusicXML file, a music21 stream, or ``voxy.score.Note``s.

  • lyrics (str | None) – One token per note (can-dle: a word over two notes; -: hold the word); default: the score’s own lyrics.

  • transpose (Union[int, Literal['fit_voice']]) – Semitones to move the melody (0: as written), or ‘fit_voice’ (into the voice’s register).

  • language (str) – Of the lyrics (SoulX here: English).

  • track (int | None) – Which line of a MIDI file to sing, when it has several (voxy.score.notes_from_midi).

  • model (SingingModel | None) – A ready model (its backend is then the backend).

voxy.singing.singing_voice(audio, score=None, lyrics=None, *, backend='soulx', sample_rate=24000, name='singing-voices', rootdir=None, **metadata)[source]¶

A singing voice: a sung clip, and (for synthesis) the score it sings.

Parameters:

  • audio (str | PathLike | bytes) – The clip, 5 to 30 s of one voice singing, without accompaniment: a path, or WAV bytes (kept in the samples store of voice name, under the data root rootdir, so the voice outlives the session).

  • score – What the clip sings, timed as in the clip (a MIDI or MusicXML file, a music21 stream, or notes; see voxy.score.read_score). Needed by sing; convert_singing needs only the clip.

  • lyrics (str | None) – Its words, if the score has none.

  • backend (str) – The backend the voice is for (its model_type).

  • **metadata – Kept with the voice (e.g. consent=, description=).

Return type:

VoiceProfile

The profile’s segment is plain data (prompt_audio path, prompt_notes), so it can be saved in the voice library: voxy.save_voice(name, voice, consent=...).
