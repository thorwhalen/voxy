voxy.lexique

voxy.lexique¶

French heteronyms derived from Lexique, to merge under voxy’s hand-made table.

Lexique (New, Pallier et al., http://www.lexique.org) lists about 140 000 French word forms with their pronunciation, lemma, part of speech and frequency. Its homographs that are not homophones (est /ɛ/ or /ɛst/, couvent /kuv/ or /kuvɑ̃/, président…) are words a speech model can misread. voxy ships a short hand-made table (voxy.pronounce.heteronym_readings) and no Lexique data, so derive more from your own copy and let the hand-made readings win:

>>> from voxy.lexique import lexique_heteronyms
>>> from voxy.pronounce import heteronym_readings
>>> derived = lexique_heteronyms("Lexique383.tsv", min_frequency=1)
>>> table = {**derived, **heteronym_readings("fr")}
>>> derived["couvent"]
{'noun': 'kuvɑ̃', 'verb': 'kuv'}

The derived table is not complete. Lexique stores one pronunciation per spelling, part of speech and lemma, and no liaison or variant readings, so heteronyms that differ only there are missing from it: tous, os, six, dix, christ, lacs, fait. It extends the hand-made table; it does not replace it.

The result has the shape voxy.pronounce takes ({word: {reading name: IPA}}, IPA in the symbols of its phoneme model). Pronunciations that differ only by Lexique’s transcription noise are one reading and its variants (verb~e); a reading is named by its part of speech, with the names of voxy.speechtext.FRENCH_READING_NAMES, or by its lemma when two readings share a part of speech:

>>> rows = [
...     {"ortho": "est", "phon": "E", "lemme": "être", "cgram": "AUX", "freqfilms2": "100", "freqlivres": "90"},
...     {"ortho": "est", "phon": "e", "lemme": "être", "cgram": "VER", "freqfilms2": "9", "freqlivres": "9"},
...     {"ortho": "est", "phon": "Est", "lemme": "est", "cgram": "NOM", "freqfilms2": "20", "freqlivres": "30"},
...     {"ortho": "chat", "phon": "Sa", "lemme": "chat", "cgram": "NOM", "freqfilms2": "40", "freqlivres": "30"},
... ]
>>> heteronyms_from_rows(rows)
{'est': {'verb': 'ɛ', 'verb~e': 'e', 'noun': 'ɛst'}}

Module Attributes

LEXIQUE_TO_IPA

Lexique's phonetic code -> IPA in the vocabulary of voxy's phoneme model (facebook/wav2vec2-lv-60-espeak-cv-ft); Lexique's other letters are that IPA.

TRANSCRIPTION_NOISE

two pronunciations equal after this are one reading (open/closed vowels, schwa).

LEXIQUE_TO_UPOS

Lexique's part of speech (cgram, whole or before its :) -> universal POS.

LEXIQUE_POS_NAMES

Lexique's part of speech -> a reading name (voxy.speechtext.FRENCH_READING_NAMES).

VERB_NAME

A pronunciation with a verb row is named verb when its heaviest part of speech is one of these: Lexique tags some verb forms as nouns or adjectives too (violent, insolent), while an adverb (plus) is genuinely heavier than its verb rows.

UNTAGGED_NAME

The name of a reading whose part of speech Lexique leaves empty.

{word: {reading name: IPA}} for the spellings Lexique reads in more than one way.

Pronunciations equal under phon_key() are one reading, its heaviest pronunciation the reading and the others <name>~<IPA> variants. Readings come heaviest first (weight: the sum of their rows’ frequencies) and are named by their heaviest part of speech (verb if any row is a verb); readings that share one are named by lemma, or <pos>:<IPA> when their lemmas coincide too. Words whose heaviest reading is under min_frequency (per million) are left out.
